Cookie Policy

Last updated: 5 October 2026

Cookies and local storage

Cookies and local storage are ways a website can remember information in your browser. CYGNUS uses browser storage to support member sign-in and membership features.

What this website stores

Supabase Auth stores a session under a project-specific key such as sb-<project>-auth-token, allowing you to remain signed in. The sac-approved-member entry caches your user ID and approval status for member navigation. Database access still depends on server-side permissions.

Analytics and third parties

The current site code does not include advertising trackers or analytics cookies. Google Fonts receives requests when fonts load. Optional Google sign-in, when enabled and selected, may use storage on Google’s own services. Those services have their own policies.

How to manage storage

Sign out from your account to end your session. You can delete this site’s cookies and local storage through your browser’s site-data settings. Clearing data or blocking storage can sign you out or prevent member features from working; public information pages remain available.

Contact

For questions about browser storage, email cygnus@btkit.org.
