FAQ

■ Most Common Questions

No worries, here you can find all the answers

Who can join CYGNUS?
+
Any student with a passion for robotics, AI, or building hardware can join CYGNUS! We welcome all skill levels, from beginners looking to learn, to advanced developers ready to lead a project.
Do I need prior experience in robotics or AI?
+
Not at all. CYGNUS provides mentorship, workshops, and team-based learning. As long as you have the curiosity and dedication to learn, you will quickly pick up the skills you need.
What kind of projects does the club build?
+
We build everything from autonomous drones and IoT smart-home devices to computer vision systems and advanced robotics. Our portfolio is shaped by the ideas our members bring to the table.
How do I pitch my own project idea?
+
Once you become a member, you can submit a project proposal during our bi-weekly incubation sessions. If approved, the club provides funding, lab space, and team support to help you build it.
When and where do we meet?
+
We will let you know according to the need !

CYGNUS

CYGNUS is the technical club at Bipin Tripathi Kumaon Institute of Technology, Dwarahat, Uttarakhand. Explore robotics, sensing, embedded systems and automation through student projects, workshops and club events.
