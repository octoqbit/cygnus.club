About these terms

These terms describe use of the CYGNUS website and member portal at BTKIT Dwarahat. Contact cygnus@btkit.org for questions about the service.

Accounts and membership

Provide accurate application details and keep your login credentials private. Applications are reviewed by club administrators; submitting one does not guarantee approval. Your email is your login ID. A department-based member ID, such as CYG-EC-001, is assigned after approval.

Responsible participation

Treat other members respectfully. Do not impersonate others, misuse personal information, attempt unauthorized access, disrupt the website, or submit unlawful or harmful material. Club administrators may restrict membership access for misuse. You may contact the club to discuss a membership decision.

Events and projects

Check each event’s published eligibility, schedule and participation instructions. Registration does not override event-specific requirements. Events and project information may change; contact the club when you need confirmation. Follow organizer instructions and appropriate safety practices during hands-on activities.

Publishing CYGNUS projects

All projects developed under CYGNUS must be published on the club website and uploaded to the club’s official GitHub organization. Members may also publish the same projects on their personal GitHub profiles.

Provide the CYGNUS team with the project summary, source code, documentation, and contributor credits needed for publication. Credit the club and contributors when sharing the project.
