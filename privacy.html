Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 October 2026

Who we are

CYGNUS is a student club at BTKIT Dwarahat, Uttarakhand, India. For privacy questions or requests, contact cygnus@btkit.org.

Information used by the member portal

When you apply, we collect your name,mobile number, email, department, year of study, interests, application message and chosen pronouns and tech emoji. We store your membership status, assigned member ID and event registrations. Supabase Auth manages login credentials and password resets; club administrators cannot view your password.

Why we use this information

We use these details to review applications, manage membership, authenticate your account, register you for events and respond to support requests. Authorized club administrators can review applications and registrations. Public club pages do not display your private application or email.

Public CYGNUS projects

All projects developed under CYGNUS must be published on the club website and uploaded to the club’s official GitHub organization. Members may also publish the same projects on their personal GitHub profiles.

Project summaries, code, documentation, and contributor credits supplied for publication are public. Keep private account details and credentials out of project submissions. See our project publishing terms.

Service providers and browser data

Supabase provides authentication, database and storage services. Our hosting provider receives technical request information to deliver and secure the site. Pages load fonts from Google Fonts, which receives connection information such as your IP address. If Google sign-in is enabled and you choose it, Google also processes your sign-in under its own privacy policy. See our Cookie Policy for browser storage details.

Retention and your choices

We keep membership and registration records while needed for club administration and applicable obligations. To request access, correction, deletion, or withdrawal of permission to use information you supplied, email cygnus@btkit.org from your registered address. We may need to verify your identity. Removing information needed for membership can prevent use of member services. You can update your name and tech emoji in your account. Your pronoun selection is fixed after submission.

Updates and concerns

We may update this notice when the service changes. The date above identifies the latest version. Send privacy concerns to cygnus@btkit.org.
